AI in Medicine: Practical Tools,
Evaluations & Teaching Guides

A curated collection of practical AI resources for clinicians, basic scientists, clinical researchers, faculty, residents, staff, and institutional leaders. These pages explain what major AI tools do, where they can help, where they can fail, and how to evaluate them before using them in clinical, educational, research, administrative, or partner-governed workflows.
